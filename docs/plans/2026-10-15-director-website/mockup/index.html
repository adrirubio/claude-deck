The idea, in pictures

Who does what

One person is in charge. The AI team members work in a clear chain under that person. The computer systems follow fixed rules — they are not bosses. One role, the Supervisor, can be an AI agent or a person.

A person An AI agent A computer system

What the factory is

The factory is everything inside the line: the Supervisor, the Deck controller and the team, working together on your backlog. You, your optional helper and the place your work lives stay outside it.
